- Size: 4:3 (landscape, the width to the height is 4 to 3).
- Draw one simple, clear scene, described in plain words. No writing, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks inside the picture.
- Prophets (peace be upon them), including Prophet Muhammad ﷺ: never show any part of a body. Show a prophet only as a complete figure of pure light, a glowing outline in the place where he stands, with no face, hands, feet, hair or skin to be seen.
- Angels: the same as a prophet. Only light, with no face and no part of a body.
- The companions of the Prophet ﷺ (RA): show them as people in plain clothes of their time, but their faces are made of light: a soft glow where the face would be, with no eyes, nose or mouth.
- Allah (SWT): never draw or show Him in any way.
- Other people (ordinary people, traders, rulers, soldiers): normal, respectful and fitting for the time and the place. Never mock a person or a faith.
- Never draw the Quran or a hadith as readable writing. Show a closed book, a glowing book or a calm scene instead.
